HOW TO SWING

Hold, fly, let go.

A left hook / D right hookWhile held, you hook onto the left (A) or right (D) row of hook points along the course. Even when flying sideways or backwards, A always grabs the left row and D the right row, further up the course. Hold A+D to hook both sides; releasing a key lets go of that cable only.

W forward / S backPush forward or back. Steer left and right by choosing which side to hook. Let go of A·D on the green signal to fly on momentum.

GatesFly through each map's 7–9 gates in order. On curves you drift outward, so pull in with the inside hook to follow the course. Falling, hitting buildings, rocks or trees, or missing a gate sends you back to the last checkpoint with +3 seconds.

R restart ESC pause ← → switch map M all mapsSwitch maps with ‹ › on the menu card or ← →, or open "All maps" (M) to search, filter by difficulty and race right away. On mobile, hold the left or right hook pad at the bottom to hook, and slide your thumb up or down while holding to push forward or back. The ▲▼ buttons in the middle work too.
